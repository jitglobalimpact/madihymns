About This App / Eguni ri App ni

Ma'di Hymns is a free digital hymnbook containing Anglican hymns in the Ma'di language. Designed for personal devotion, congregational worship, choir use, and Anglican clergy across Ma'diland and the West Nile region of Uganda.

How to Use / Mana ri Ting'o ni

Browse hymns by number or title. Use the search bar to find any hymn quickly. Tap a hymn to read the full lyrics. Adjust text size using the Aa buttons. Share any hymn with your congregation directly via WhatsApp.

Install on Your Phone / Tia ki Foni ni

Open this app in Chrome on your Android phone. Tap the menu (⋮) and select "Add to Home Screen". The app will install like a normal app and works offline.
