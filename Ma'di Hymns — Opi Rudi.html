About / Eguni ri App ni

Ma'di Hymns is a free digital hymnbook containing Anglican hymns in the Ma'di language. For personal devotion, congregational worship, choir use, and clergy across Ma'diland and West Nile.

How to Use / Mana ri Ting'o ni

Browse hymns by number or title. Search to find any hymn quickly. Tap a hymn to read full lyrics. Adjust text size with Aa buttons. Share via WhatsApp.

Install on Phone / Tia ki Foni ni

Open in Chrome on Android. Tap menu ⋮ and select "Add to Home Screen". Works offline after first visit.
